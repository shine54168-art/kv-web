Who we are

From Singapore, built for Taiwan

HiReKing's roots are in Singapore - a market where executive search is run as a professional service, with defined process, compliance and client-data governance. We brought that framework to Taiwan, but we did not simply copy the Singapore playbook.

Taiwan's semiconductor and advanced manufacturing sector has its own rhythm: shift culture in fabs and OSAT plants, the commuting radius around the Hsinchu and Tainan science parks, the weight of stock bonuses and year-end pay in every negotiation. Only people who have recruited here understand that. So every consultant on the Taiwan team came from a leading Taiwanese search firm.

What the combination actually changes

  • Vetting is not a formality - credentials, reasons for leaving and non-compete exposure are checked item by item, to HQ standard.
  • Data protection is built into the process - collection, use and retention of candidate data follow explicit rules, not a consultant's personal laptop.
  • Conflict of interest is written down - we do not source from clients we serve, and the scope and duration are agreed up front.
  • Local judgement stays local - how a role is framed and how pay is negotiated is decided by consultants who know this market.

HR Consulting

Once people are in, does your HR system hold?

Recruitment solves an empty seat. The deeper problem is usually retention, management capability and pay that nobody can explain. Our consulting line covers exactly that: job architecture and pay structure, performance systems, labour compliance, employee relations and attrition analysis.

  • Organisation and job architecture review — role mapping, grading, salary bands and market benchmarking.
  • Labour compliance review — working hours, overtime, leave, contracts and work rules.
  • Employee relations and attrition analysis — structured exit interviews that surface the real cause.
  • HR process design — rebuilding the flow from hiring and onboarding through review and exit.

How we work

From the first call to the first day

The process is transparent. Timelines and responsibilities are agreed up front, and you always know which stage the search is at.

01

Briefing and role definition

We do more than take the JD. Why does this role exist, why did the last person leave, what does the hiring manager actually expect, and what is the real salary band? If the brief is unrealistic we will say so — an impossible brief never closes.

02

Search strategy and talent mapping

We define target companies and titles, approach passive candidates directly, and report back what the market actually supplies and what it costs.

03

Screening and in-depth interviews

Every shortlisted candidate is interviewed by a consultant. You receive motivation, reasons for leaving, salary expectations and any risks we see — never a forwarded CV.

04

Interviews and offer negotiation

We coordinate interviews, collect feedback both ways, and manage the offer stage — pay, start date and counter-offer risk.

05

Onboarding follow-up and guarantee

We check in after the start date. If the hire does not work out within the guarantee period, we replace them at no additional fee — and we tell you what we noticed about the organisation along the way.

What employers ask us most

A fair question, and we will not dodge it: the HiReKing Taiwan team is genuinely new. But what is new is the brand, not the people. Every consultant on the Taiwan team came from a leading Taiwanese recruitment or search firm, and the assignments they have run and the networks they have built came with them.

The other half is the framework: we operate on our Singapore parent's standards, with written rules for candidate vetting, data protection and conflict of interest. In practice a new brand carries one real advantage - we have no existing commitments to your competitors, so no part of the market is off-limits to our search.

If you are still unsure, the sensible test is to give us one role and judge us on the outcome rather than on our incorporation date.
