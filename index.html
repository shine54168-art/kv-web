Facing extreme time pressure, KV's team delivered a complete technical integration platform within one quarter, bridging foreign HQ equipment systems with a local semiconductor manufacturer's production data. Our edge lay in rapidly clarifying client needs and system constraints, designing a multi-protocol, real-time monitoring data pipeline across 80+ device integration specs. After 3–4 rounds of rigorous testing and stress validation, we delivered a stable production system — proving KV's capability to precisely deliver mid-to-large system development under tight deadlines.

Digital Transformation for a Major Retail Group

To support digital transformation, a renowned retail group sought to integrate internal systems and build a new data platform. The project spanned two tracks: first, building a data middle-platform to connect internal business systems and rebuild the consumer experience. KV led the core architecture and implementation, delivering a platform integrating membership, orders, and inventory with real-time e-commerce integration. The second phase focused on an analytics module with dashboards and automated reporting. Through dual lenses of "system audit" and "data modeling," KV helped define a clear technical blueprint, ultimately delivering a stable data platform that drove the transformation.

To improve customer service efficiency amid business growth, a prominent digital bank engaged KV to rebuild an integrated CS platform and optimize team operations. Drawing on our fintech and organizational consulting expertise, both sides confirmed specs and kicked off development within six weeks. The project ran in two phases: first, digitizing CS workflows — KV built a platform integrating ticket management, knowledge base, and live chat, connected to the bank's existing CRM; second, from an organizational consulting angle, KV conducted a process health check and employee relations diagnosis for the CS team, redesigning roles and performance systems to boost efficiency and retention. Beyond delivery, KV helped the client advance on both technical and organizational fronts.

FAQ

服務常見問題

Frequently Asked Questions

KV 與其他軟體開發或顧問公司有什麼差別？How is KV different from other firms?

KV 同時具備軟體開發與組織顧問兩種能力。我們不只是寫程式，更能從業務與組織角度理解問題；也不只出建議報告，更能親手把系統與制度落地。這種「診斷 + 開發」一條龍的整合能力，是我們最大的差異。

KV combines software development and organizational consulting. We don't just code — we understand problems from a business and organizational angle; and we don't just deliver recommendation decks — we implement systems and processes ourselves. This end-to-end "diagnose + build" capability is our key difference.

KV 的收費方式是如何計算的？How does KV charge for its services?

依服務類型而定。軟體開發與系統整合採「固定範疇、固定報價」，依專案規模與工作量估價；組織顧問服務則依顧問投入（人日／人時）或專案範疇計費。雙方於合約中明確約定範疇、里程碑與付款條件。

It depends on the service type. Software development and system integration use "fixed scope, fixed quote" based on project size; organizational consulting is billed by consulting effort (daily/hourly) or project scope. Scope, milestones, and payment terms are clearly defined in the contract.

一個專案的合作流程是怎麼進行的？What does the engagement process look like?

收到需求後，KV 啟動跨職能協作流程：先由團隊進行需求訪談與現況診斷，釐清業務目標與痛點；接著提出技術或組織方案、確認範疇與里程碑；開發或導入階段以敏捷迭代推進，定期交付可驗證成果；最後進行測試上線或成效驗收。專案結束後，我們持續提供保固維護與優化建議，確保成果穩定運行。

Upon receiving requirements, KV activates a cross-functional process: requirement interviews and diagnosis to clarify goals and pain points; then a technical or organizational proposal with defined scope and milestones; agile iterations with regular verifiable deliverables during build or rollout; and finally testing, launch, or outcome acceptance. After completion, we provide warranty maintenance and optimization advice to keep results running stably.

專案交付後是否提供保固與維護？Do you offer post-delivery warranty?

是的。KV 提供「交付保固機制」——系統上線後，於約定的保固期內，我們將依合約條款免費修復屬於開發範疇內的程式缺陷，並提供技術支援。詳細保固天數與條款可參閱我們的示範合約。

Yes. KV offers a delivery warranty — after launch, within the agreed warranty period, we fix in-scope code defects free of charge and provide technical support per contract terms. See our sample contract for details.

Project-based services are typically invoiced by milestone (signing, design, development, acceptance); long-term advisory settles by period. Upon invoice, clients pay within the agreed period; late payments incur interest per contract.
