知名數位金融銀行，重整客服團隊

Restructuring Customer Service at a Leading Digital Bank

為提升客服部門整體表現並因應主管離任，某知名數位銀行決定對外尋訪新任客服主管並重整內部團隊。憑藉 KV 在金融產業的深厚經驗，該銀行在一個半月內順利延攬一位具備外商銀行背景的資深客服主管。新主管上任後立即啟動雙階段重組計畫——第一階段針對客服團隊自然流失造成的人力缺口，KV 協助招募 6 位資深客服人才，並由新主管親自面談篩選，有效提升團隊整體效能；第二階段面對擴編需求與正式編制限制，KV 建議導入派遣制度作為短期過渡方案，並從中挑選表現優異者於正式編制釋出時轉為正職，最終為客戶引入 9 位派遣客服。本案 KV 不僅完成尋訪任務，更協助客戶建立外包人員的薪酬與管理框架，大幅提升組織人力的靈活度。

To improve customer service performance amid leadership change, a prominent digital bank engaged KV to source a new department head and restructure the team. Drawing on our financial industry depth, the bank onboarded a seasoned CS leader with foreign-bank credentials within six weeks. The new leader launched a two-phase plan: first, KV recruited 6 experienced CS specialists to fill natural attrition gaps; second, given headcount constraints, KV proposed a contract-staffing model with conversion pathways, ultimately placing 9 contract specialists. Beyond search delivery, KV also helped the client design compensation and management frameworks for contingent staff — significantly enhancing organizational agility.

FAQ

尋訪顧問服務常見問題

Frequently Asked Questions

KV 人才尋訪與其他顧問有什麼差別？How is KV different from other consultancies?

KV 的尋訪顧問團隊由資深產業專家組成，深耕 AI、數位金融、行銷科技與 Web3 等前沿科技領域。我們不僅倚賴深厚的全球人脈與封閉式人才智庫，更導入 AI 與行銷科技工具進行精準匹配，協助企業在最短時間內鎖定關鍵人才，大幅縮短決策週期。

KV's consultant team comprises senior industry experts deeply rooted in AI, Fintech, Martech, and Web3. We combine global networks and exclusive talent pools with AI and Martech tools for precise matching — helping enterprises identify key talent quickly and shorten decision cycles.

請問 KV 人才尋訪服務是在成功媒合後收費嗎？Does KV only charge upon successful placement?

是的。我們採取「成功媒合制」——僅在推薦的候選人正式到職後，才會依合約條款收取顧問服務費，與企業共同承擔尋才風險。

Yes. We operate on a success-based model — fees are only charged after the recommended candidate officially onboards, sharing the recruitment risk with our clients.

KV 如何幫助我招募到適合的人選？How does KV help find the right candidate?

收到企業的人才需求後，KV 立即啟動跨部門協同尋訪流程：商業顧問部展開全景式人才搜尋；客戶服務部針對候選人進行深度面談與背景核實；行銷團隊在專業社群中精準觸及潛在人選；產業研究部門同步進行同業薪酬與人才競爭力分析。針對企業屬意的候選人，我們依需求量身打造篩選流程，涵蓋技能評測、多輪面試與資歷查核，並在條件談判階段協助薪酬斡旋。媒合成功後，我們持續提供保證期內的跟進服務，確保候選人順利融入團隊。

Upon receiving your requirements, KV activates a cross-departmental sourcing process: business consultants launch panoramic search, client services conduct in-depth interviews and verification, marketing reaches potential candidates in professional communities, and research handles compensation and competitiveness analysis. For preferred candidates, we tailor the screening process with skill assessments, multi-round interviews, and reference checks, plus negotiation support. After placement, we provide ongoing follow-up during the guarantee period.

請問 KV 顧問服務是否提供試用期保證？Does KV offer probation period guarantees?

是的。KV 提供「保證期遞補機制」——若候選人在保證期內離職或不適任，我們將依合約條款，於約定期限內提供替補候選人。詳細保證期天數與條款可參閱我們的示範合約。

Yes. KV offers a guarantee period replacement mechanism — if a placement departs or proves unsuitable within the guarantee period, we provide a replacement candidate per contract terms. See our sample contract for details.
