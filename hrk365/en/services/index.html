Recruitment & workforce services

Four hiring models — pick the right one first

A vacancy can be filled by search, by dispatch, by RPO or by outsourcing — and the cost structure, employer liability and speed are nothing alike. This page sorts them out.

Talk it through with a consultant →

What we do

Permanent Placement

For key roles, leadership hires and scarce specialists. Success-based fee with a replacement guarantee.

  • Direct approach to passive candidates, not just inbound applications
  • Consultant-led interviews with a written risk assessment
  • Offer negotiation and counter-offer management
Learn more →

Contract & Temporary Staffing

For projects, peak seasons, parental cover and headcount freezes. Employer duties and payroll sit with us.

  • We are the employer of record for insurance and payroll
  • Headcount flexes with the project
  • Conversion to permanent can be agreed up front
Learn more →

Volume Hiring & RPO

For new lines, new sites, seasonal intakes — or handing over the hiring process end to end.

  • Project-based or an embedded long-term team
  • Group assessment days and onboarding handled together
  • Weekly reporting on progress and fill rate
Learn more →

Payroll & Statutory Outsourcing

For companies without dedicated HR, or those moving repetitive admin and compliance risk off their plate.

  • Payroll runs, overtime and leave deductions
  • Labour and health insurance, pension and supplementary premium filings
  • Payslip delivery and annual withholding statements
Learn more →
Choosing

How the four compare

When in doubt, start from where you want employer liability to sit. That question usually settles it.

PermanentContractVolume / RPOPayroll
Best forKey, leadership and scarce specialist rolesProjects, peaks, cover rolesMany similar roles at onceYou have the people; the admin is the problem
Who employsYou — the hire joins your payrollUs, as the dispatching employerYouYou — we administer only
PricingSuccess fee, a share of the hire's salaryMonthly or per headProject fee or monthly retainerMonthly, by headcount and scope
SpeedWeeks to months, depending on scarcityFastest — short-term coverBatched and run in parallelLive from the month after handover
Guarantee / flexibilityFree replacement within the guaranteeHeadcount flexes with demandAdjusted by contract if fill targets are missedScope can expand in stages

What we typically report back

  • "This band sits roughly 15% below market, so you are only attracting candidates without strong motivation to move."
  • "Three interview rounds took six weeks; the first two candidates accepted elsewhere in week five."
  • "The same work carries three different titles here, so candidates cannot see a career path."
The difference

Every service comes with the consulting view attached

Whatever we see while running your search — pay bands drifting from market, an interview process so long candidates drop out, grading so unclear that offers are hard to defend — we report back rather than quietly working around it. It is why many clients end up handing us the consulting work too.
