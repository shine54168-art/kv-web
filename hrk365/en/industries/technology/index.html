Market reality

Engineers are not short of jobs — they are short of reasons

A capable senior engineer in Taiwan usually has two or three live options. What decides a move is rarely a few thousand more per month — it is whether the technical problem is interesting, whether the manager can actually lead, and how deep the team's technical debt runs.

So when we present your role we will ask you to be specific: what the team really builds, who makes the technical calls, the release cadence and the on-call load. Candidates will ask all of it, and vagueness just wastes an interview slot.

What we prepare for you

  • Market salary ranges for the level, and what offers have actually closed recently
  • Comparable roles your competitors have open, and what they are offering
  • The concerns candidates raise about your role, including why some decline
  • Concrete feedback where a long interview process cost you a candidate
