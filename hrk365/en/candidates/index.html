For candidates

Your CV does not go anywhere without you

To be clear: HiReKing is engaged by employers to fill specific roles, so the opportunities we can offer depend on our current assignments. We do not forward CVs, and nothing is sent anywhere without your agreement.

What you can expect from us

Your data is governed, and you know where it goes

We ask you before your details go to any company, for any role. Under our Singapore parent's rules, the purpose, scope and retention period for your data are defined — and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

You talk to a consultant, not a database

Our consultants came from Taiwan's leading search firms and understand your technical background. The conversation is about whether the move makes sense for you — not a recital of the job requirements.

Straight information about the role

Shift patterns, overtime intensity, the state of the team and why the role is open — we tell you what we know. A surprise after you start serves neither of us.

We will tell you when it is not a fit

If there is a real gap between your profile and the role, we say so and explain why — rather than sending you to an interview and spending your leave on a long shot.

Straight talk

What we can and cannot do for you

A search firm is not a job-application service. Our assignments come from employers, so the opportunities we have are those roles — and if nothing current fits you, no CV, however strong, changes that.

That does not make it pointless to talk to us. We keep your details on file and get in touch when something fits. And you can get an honest market read from us in the meantime: what your profile is worth right now, which skill combinations are in demand, and where to strengthen next. That conversation is free.

How to reach us

  • Email your CV to us directly

You do not have to be looking to talk to us

Opportunities in semiconductor and technology move fast. Knowing a consultant who understands your field means one more option on the day you decide to move.
