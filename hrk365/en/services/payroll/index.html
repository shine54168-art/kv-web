Payroll and statutory outsourcing

Get payroll wrong once and trust takes the hit. It is repetitive, fiddly and unforgiving — which makes it the single best thing to hand over.

Ask for a quote →

What is included

Payroll

  • Monthly, hourly and bonus calculations
  • Overtime, shift allowances and additional payments
  • Leave, lateness and deductions
  • Payslip production and delivery

Statutory insurance & pension

  • Enrolment and withdrawal for labour, health and employment insurance
  • Bracket adjustments and change filings
  • Employer 6% pension contributions and voluntary contributions
  • Supplementary health premium calculation and filing

Filings & reporting

  • Withholding on payments and annual withholding statements
  • Payroll cost analysis and reporting by department
  • Year-end bonuses and payment in lieu of unused annual leave
  • Preparing records for labour inspections
Why hand it over

What you save is not only time — it is exposure

Payroll and statutory administration in Taiwan is full of detail: when insurance brackets must be adjusted, what counts as the base for overtime, how unused annual leave is converted, the threshold for supplementary health premiums. The rules change, and missing one can mean back-payments, fines and a loss of trust with staff.

In companies without dedicated HR this work usually lands on someone in accounting or admin — a corner of the business nobody is confident about and nobody is allowed to get wrong. Handing it over is one of the easiest outsourcing decisions to justify.

When this fits

  • You are growing but do not yet have dedicated HR
  • Payroll currently sits with accounting — or with you
  • You run shifts, overtime or several different pay structures
  • Staff have queried their pay, or you have had to recalculate
  • You are a foreign company setting up in Taiwan and need local compliance handled
