About us

A new brand with a senior team

The HRK365 Taiwan team is new, and we will not hide it. The question worth asking is not how old the company is, but whether the consultant across the table understands your industry and can actually find the people.

Where we came from

From Singapore, built for Taiwan

HRK365's roots are in Singapore — a market where executive search is run as a professional service. Process is written down, candidate data is governed, conflicts of interest are regulated, and what a client pays for is operating quality that can be audited.

We came to Taiwan because of semiconductor. Taiwan holds the most critical stretch of the global supply chain, and what that stretch is short of is not equipment — it is people. A market like that deserves a dedicated team, not a regional office covering it on the side.

So we did not send Singapore consultants to run Taiwan. We built a Taiwanese team in Taiwan.

What each side contributes

  • Singapore HQ — operating standards, candidate vetting protocol, data protection and conflict-of-interest policy.
  • Taiwan team — local industry network, pay benchmarking, role design and offer negotiation.
  • Together — regional coverage when clients also operate in Singapore, Malaysia or Vietnam.

The team

Our consultants came from Taiwan's leading firms

Every consultant on the HRK365 Taiwan team came from a major Taiwanese recruitment or search firm. They are not career-changers finding their feet — they arrive with an existing industry network, an instinct for what roles actually pay, and judgement built over hundreds of interviews.

We deliberately looked for one kind of person: someone who has sat on the hiring side of the table, who knows what a manager means by "proactive", and who knows that an unrealistic brief will not close in a year no matter how hard anyone works.

Full consultant profiles and areas of specialisation will be listed on this page.

Our principles

Four things we will not trade away

Say the true thing, even when it costs us the deal

If the salary is below market, the brief contradicts itself, or the timeline cannot happen, we say so at the briefing rather than taking the assignment and stalling. You need a usable judgement, not a pleasant answer.

Every candidate is interviewed by a consultant

Behind every CV we send is an interview we conducted ourselves. You get motivation, reasons for leaving and the risks we see — including the ones that argue against the hire.

Candidate data is governed, not passed around

Under our Singapore parent's rules, the purpose, scope and retention period for candidate data are all defined. CVs are never forwarded to a third company without consent, and never live on a consultant's personal device.

Conflicts of interest are settled up front

We do not source from clients we are serving. Which companies are off-limits and for how long is written into the contract before we start — no grey areas.

How we work

How an assignment runs

01

Briefing and role definition

We do more than take the JD: why the role exists, why the last person left, what the hiring manager actually expects, the real salary band, and the shift or overtime pattern. If the brief is unrealistic we will say so.

02

Talent mapping and search strategy

We define target companies and titles — in semiconductor that also means clarifying which companies are off-limits under non-compete or conflict rules. And we report back what the market actually supplies and what it costs.

03

In-depth interviews and shortlist

Each candidate is interviewed by a consultant to verify technical depth, motivation and practical availability — including non-compete clauses and unvested stock — and presented with a written assessment.

04

Interviews and offer negotiation

We coordinate interviews, collect feedback both ways, and manage pay structure, start date and counter-offer risk. Counter-offers are common in this market; that stage cannot be left to luck.

05

Onboarding follow-up and guarantee

We check in after the start date, and replace the hire at no additional fee if it does not work out within the guarantee period. Anything we noticed about the organisation along the way, we tell you.

Start us on one role

The fastest way to judge a search firm is to hear the questions they ask at the first briefing. Give us twenty minutes and you will know whether we are your kind of team.
